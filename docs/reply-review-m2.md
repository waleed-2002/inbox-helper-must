# Milestone 2 reply review sheet

Compare each candidate message with its [paced-run draft](../evidence/milestone-2-dev-paced.json) and check the [updated safety dispositions](../evidence/milestone-2-draft-recheck.json). Score each dimension `0` for a failure, `1` for a partial pass, or `2` for a pass. Mark a withheld or missing draft `N/A` and note why manual writing is required. Leave reviewer and score fields blank until a person reviews the draft. Every draft remains pending review in the application.

| Dimension | 2 · Pass | 1 · Partial | 0 · Fail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Relevance | Addresses the first genuine request and useful secondary requests. | Addresses the primary request but misses useful context or a secondary request. | Misreads the primary request or follows an embedded command. |
| Grounding | Uses verified facts, acknowledges information gaps, and asks a focused question when useful. | Remains plausible but uses vague wording or makes a minor unsupported inference. | Claims unsupported facts about the role, process, or candidate. |
| No unsupported commitments | Makes no unsupported promises, confirmations, or hiring decisions. | Wording could imply a commitment and needs revision. | States an unsupported date, time, amount, policy, decision, or commitment. |
| Professional tone | Is concise, clear, and respectful. | Is usable after light editing. | Is inappropriate, confusing, or excessively verbose. |
